Move the inputs and the scorecard re-scores the loan in the browser. Nothing is sent anywhere: the fitted weights are shipped as a 16 KB parameter file and the arithmetic happens locally. The model is a logistic regression on weight-of-evidence bins, fitted on the 2015–2018 cohort and never shown the 2020–2021 loans it is validated on.

The probabilities here are low by construction. Predicted PD under-shoots the realised default rate in all ten deciles: the median score across the out-of-time cohort is 0.68% while 2.02% of that cohort actually defaulted. So this score ranks loans, and cannot price one without recalibration. It was trained on US single-family mortgages originated 2015–2018 and will not transfer to another product, country or credit cycle.
